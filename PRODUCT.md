# PRODUCT.md

- **Product:** Fuse, a personal hardware and robotics learning diary where skills heat up only from proof.
- **Audience:** one student maker (Josef) and, through the export, university admissions readers.
- **Mode:** Operate (app), Experience and Read (export).
- **Voice:** plain, short, sentence case, verb-first buttons, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
- **Principles:** heat is data; calm by default; one main thing per screen; nothing is hard-deleted; the user's words are never rewritten.
- **Anti-goals:** glow and gradients as decoration; dashboards of everything at once; AI generating the user's writing.
